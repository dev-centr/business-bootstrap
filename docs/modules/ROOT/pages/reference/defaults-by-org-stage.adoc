= Defaults by org stage
:description: Recommended email and infra defaults from solo founder through small team.
:navtitle: Defaults by stage

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
| Stage | Email defaults | Infra

| Solo / pre-product
| Cloudflare Routing inbound; ZeptoMail or defer human SMTP; Resend when first app mail ships
| Optional personal infra; else DNS by hand until painful

| Product shipping
| Resend on `updates.` (or similar); Customer.io when journeys needed; ZeptoMail for human `@domain`
| Org `infra` Pulumi repo; encode Resend/Zepto/DMARC DNS

| Small team (collaborators)
| Per-user ZeptoMail keys; never shared master SMTP; escalate DMARC after alignment
| Central `infra` + Antora docs; secrets in ESC/vault

| Many seats / compliance
| Consider Google Workspace for inboxes; keep Resend/CIO for product
| Hybrid IaC; policy as code; marketing subdomain isolation mandatory
|===
