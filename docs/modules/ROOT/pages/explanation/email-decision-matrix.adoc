= Email decision matrix
:description: Choose email providers by job — human correspondence vs product transactional vs marketing journeys.
:navtitle: Email decision matrix

Use this matrix when picking vendors. Defaults are de-facto standards for Dev-Centr–aligned orgs; alternatives are valid when the job changes.

== By job

[cols="1,1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
| Job | Default | Strong alternatives | Avoid as default

| Inbound to personal Gmail
| Cloudflare Email Routing (free)
| Google Workspace / Zoho Mail seats
| Using a transactional ESP as an inbox

| Human outbound via Gmail "Send mail as"
| **ZeptoMail** SMTP + per-user keys; SPF/DKIM in Cloudflare
| SMTP2GO; Workspace native SMTP
| **Resend** (keep for app/code only); Brevo free/starter for multi-user; shared "master" SMTP password; Gmail's own SMTP with custom From under `p=reject`

| App hard transactional
| **Resend** (DX)
| ZeptoMail (PAYG + Mail Agents if already standardized); Postmark; SES
| Marketing ESP for critical mail; Resend for human Gmail send-as

| Product marketing + behavioral journeys

| Braze / Klaviyo at scale
| Brevo when you need event-driven multi-channel

| Classic email marketing (lists/campaigns, little product orchestration)

| Customer.io (overkill if email-only)
| Resend Broadcasts dual-running with another marketing brain

| Ultra-cheap high volume, AWS-native

| —

| Full business inboxes at scale
| Google Workspace (or Zoho Mail seats)

== Scoring dimensions

Rate providers against:

* **Collaborator seats on cheap tiers** — Brevo weak; ZeptoMail / Resend / Customer.io stronger
* **Setup UX** — ZeptoMail high; SES low
* **Gmail send-as fit** — SMTP credentials + static DKIM; watch IP allowlisting traps (Brevo banner)
* **Product API / SDK DX** — Resend high
* **Journey / multi-channel** — Customer.io high; Brevo medium as ESP; ZeptoMail none
* **Pricing cliff** — ZeptoMail PAYG flat; Resend/CIO have plan steps; Brevo seats
* **Isolation model** — ZeptoMail Mail Agents; CIO workspaces; Brevo sub-orgs paywalled
* **IaC maturity** — Cloudflare DNS: Pulumi native; ZeptoMail: Dynamic Provider or manual DNS tokens; Brevo: Terraform bridge possible
* **Reputation separation** — marketing subdomain vs transactional From

== Customer.io vs Brevo

Not a coin flip:

* **Customer.io** when the product emits events and you need journeys across email/SMS/push/in-app.
* **Brevo** when you mainly need a classic ESP (campaigns/lists/templates), accept collaboration paywalls on cheap tiers, and do not need deep product orchestration.
