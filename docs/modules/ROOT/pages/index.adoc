= Business Bootstrap
:description: Support-systems hub for tech companies — email, scheduling, design, vaults, chat, funding, and org-level IaC.

Hub for founders and ops teams setting up essential **IT support systems** and related **business ops** topics (there is no separate business-resources Antora site). Capability areas hold the deep articles; the stack map and **stage defaults** stitch them together. Prefer **hosted** until ops cost or residency forces self-host. Not every area is day-one — follow xref:reference/defaults-by-org-stage.adoc[Defaults by org stage].
